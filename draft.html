Two places a partnership went quiet

Both are the same bug: somebody presses a button, the database does exactly the right thing, and the page comes back looking identical. Accept → first session and completed session → next session are the two biggest drops between signing up and studying weekly, and neither of them was a missing feature — they were presses that ended nowhere.

Everything below is the live markup and the live stylesheet, in the states the app now produces. Nothing is booked by anything here: every path still ends in a proposal the other person has to accept.

1 · Somebody accepts your request

The accept now writes a real notification to the person who asked — the same table every session event goes through, so it survives the tab closing and it is emailed like the rest. The link in it is app.html?plan=<their id>, which opens the booking form with them already in it.

Before: the row changed status. The person who asked found out the next time they happened to open PeerFlow and look at the bell, and the person who accepted was handed back the directory of strangers they had just stopped needing.

What this did not touch

No new tables and no migration — the accept notification goes through notify_partner(), which has been in the database since the MVP migration and was called from nowhere. Attendance, reliability, the no-show policy, the standing-slot rules and messaging are all unchanged, and their tests were run to say so rather than assumed. Rescheduling, dormancy nudges and scheduled reminders are deliberately not here.
