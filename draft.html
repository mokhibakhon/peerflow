The session has a shape, and a sound

Two things to look at. The pill in the bar is the pomodoro, and the three cards below are ambience. Both are live: the pill is driven by assets/phases.js, the real module, and the sounds are generated in the browser rather than downloaded.

1. The pomodoro, derived rather than synchronised

Nothing here is broadcast between the two of you. call-token already hands both browsers the same starts_at, so the phase is a function of the clock and cannot disagree between screens — no host, no messages, and somebody who joins twenty minutes late sees the right phase with the right time left, immediately. The cost is real and worth saying out loud: it cannot be paused, because there would be nowhere to put the fact that it was.

Your three session lengths turn out to already be pomodoro shapes, which is why this needs no new setting: 25 is one sitting, 50 is 25·5·20, 80 is 25·5·25·5·20.

The pill takes the corner the raw “23 min in” clock has now — same place, more use. The rail under it is the whole session at a glance. Note that a break never touches your mic or camera: doing something surprising to somebody’s hardware because a timer elapsed is not a trade worth making.

2. Ambience, local to you and generated on the spot

None of this is mixed into the call. Rain is broadband noise, which is exactly what the other side’s echo cancellation and noise suppression exist to destroy — routed into the microphone it would sound clean to you and mangled to them, which is a horrible fault to chase. So each of you picks your own, or none, and it never leaves your machine.

These are synthesised from filtered noise rather than downloaded: no megabytes on a site where nothing is content-hashed, and no loop seam to hear. Honest limit — this does rain, wind and deep noise well, and it will never do a café or a fireplace. Those need recordings, and are worth adding only if you want them.

Rain

Filtered noise with slow gusts moving through it. The most textured of the three, and the one that most sounds like a place.

Deep noise

Brown noise — the low, even one. No features to notice, which is the point: it covers a room without giving you anything to listen to.

Wind

A band of noise drifting slowly up and down. Airier than the other two, and the easiest to leave on for an hour.

What I would ship: the pill exactly as above, and these three behind the Sound button in the bar, with the choice remembered in localStorage per person rather than per session so it never asks twice. Say if you want a fourth texture, a different split than 25·5·20, or the break to be louder about itself.
