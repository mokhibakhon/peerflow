I would take C — and I had written “A” here before I looked at it rendered, which is the reason this page exists rather than a paragraph describing them.

A has a problem you can only see on the card. Green, bold and small is already what a link looks like in this rail — .rail-l is green, 700, 13px, and there is one at the foot of every card. So “You, going strong” in treatment A reads as something you can click, and it isn't. C is far enough up in size that the confusion doesn't arise.

C also happens to be what you asked for: another colour and another weight. The worry about it competing with the figure turned out not to be real — 17px against a 32px number is not a contest, and the number keeps the eye. B is the one to pick if you would rather the card never used colour to say anything, which is a defensible position; it is the only one of the three that reads the same in all three states.
