The menu — five ways

The sentence is the same in all five. Only what happens when you press a green word changes. Press Saturday 1 Aug in each one — the day menu shows the differences best, then try the time.

1Rows — what's there now

A list with a small caps heading, the date on the right, a divider before the escape hatch. For reference.

2Tiles — dates you can see, not read

Days become little date squares four across, so a date is a shape rather than a line of text. Lengths and people get the same treatment.

3Clean — no small caps, no dividers, a tick

Bigger rows, hairlines between groups instead of coloured bands, and a check mark on the one that's chosen. Nothing shouts.

4Inline — nothing floats over anything

The choices open inside the card, under the sentence, and push the rest down. No layer covering the note field, no menu running off the bottom on a phone.

5Sheet — a proper dialog

Dims the page and centres a titled card. On a phone it slides up from the bottom, thumb-height, the way a native app does it.
