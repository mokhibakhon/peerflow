Option D — four sizes

Same sentence, same menus. Only the type scale and how the pickable words are marked changes. Press the green bits in each.

1Quiet prose — reads like a line in the card

Body size, normal weight. The picks are just words with a hairline under them. The smallest this can be while still looking pressable.

2Soft pills — obviously tappable

Same body size, but each choice sits in a pale green pill. No guessing which words do something — worth a lot on a phone, where nobody hovers.

3Statement — the current one, brought down

19px instead of 23. Still the biggest thing in the card, but no longer competing with the page heading.

4Inline fields — a form that happens to read as a sentence

The picks look like small controls. Most obvious of the four, least like prose.
