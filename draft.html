Saying what is booked, and what is only being asked

Two things on the Today page read as more settled and more restricted than they are. Both are wording rather than behaviour — nothing below changes what the app can do, only what it claims.

The second one is worth reading before you pick anything: you can already propose any time on any day. The time menu has an Any other time section listing every hour from 7am to 11pm, and the day menu has a date box for any date at all. Nothing is restricted to the hours you share. The menu just puts those first and gives them more weight, which reads as "these are the options" rather than "these are the likely ones".

One thing I noticed and did not change

In your screenshot the band names Gulsanam and the form underneath says Meet Asalxon. The code points the form at the same partner the band names — pickPartner(target.i) runs immediately before the band is written, and there is a comment saying why: "The band and the form have to be about the same person and the same hour, or Propose this time opens a form offering a different one."

So either you opened the form with Someone else and changed the name yourself, which is the boring explanation — or the thing that comment is guarding against is happening anyway. Worth knowing which. If you did not touch the name dropdown, say so and I will go and find it, because that is a real bug rather than a wording one.
