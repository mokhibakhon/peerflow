How it works

Nothing decides for you. You pick the person, you both agree the time.

Step one

Find someone

Everyone on your path, closest to what you're learning first. Send a request — they can say yes.

Step two

Agree a time

The days you're both free come first. Nothing is booked until your partner accepts.

Step three

Meet

Cameras on, one hour, just the two of you. The link opens fifteen minutes before.
