Where people get to

Each step is the number of people who ever reached it, so every one is a subset of the step above and the gaps are real drop-off. It counts people rather than sessions on purpose: a session is two rows, one per person, so counting rows would report two people for every meeting between two.

The last thirty days

Each column is one day. Its height is how many accounts were made; the green is how many of those went on to finish signing up. A launch shows up here as one tall column, and whether it was worth doing shows up as how much of that column is green.
