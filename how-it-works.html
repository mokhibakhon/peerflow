How PeerFlow works

No course to buy, no curriculum to follow. PeerFlow gives your self-study the two things it's missing: direction and company.

1

Pick your career track

Eight career tracks, from frontend to AI. Your track sets the topics you'll see, the sessions you're shown first, and the peers you're matched with. Not sure yet? Browse what each career actually studies day to day — and switch tracks anytime, nothing is locked.

2

Get matched with peers

Tell PeerFlow your level (beginner, intermediate, advanced) and when you're usually free. You'll get suggested study partners and small groups of 3–6 on your track, at your level, in your timezone. Small groups beat pairs — they survive when one person has a busy week.

3

Study with me, live

Join a session from the board or start your own. Declare one goal, start the shared timer, and put in fifty focused minutes beside people who want the same job you do. Then a short break, and — if you're on a roll — one more block.

The ritual

Inside a session

Every session, silent or collaborative, follows the same simple ritual.

→

Declare a goal

One sentence, visible to the room: "Finish bandit 12–15." "Ship the navbar." "Clean the sales dataset." Vague goals don't survive being written down — that's the point.

◷

Focus with the timer

A shared 50-minute timer keeps the whole room in the same rhythm. Cameras optional, mics off in silent sessions. You're not being watched — you're being accompanied.

✓

Check out honestly

When the timer ends: did you do what you came to do? Your streak counts finished goals and attended sessions — not hours the tab was open.
