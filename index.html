How it works

How PeerFlow works

Three steps between you and a study crew that actually shows up.

1

Pick your career track

Eight careers, from frontend to AI. Not sure yet? Browse what each one actually studies day to day, and switch anytime.

2

Get matched with peers

PeerFlow suggests study partners and small groups of 3–6 on your track, at your level, in your timezone — people who fit your real schedule.

3

Study with me, live

Join a session, declare one goal, start the timer. Fifty minutes of focus beside people who want the same job you do, then a short break.
