How PeerFlow works

1

Tell us what you're learning

The topic, roughly where you're at, and the hours you're actually free. One minute.

2

We find you one person

Not a group. Not a room. One person learning the same thing, at a similar stage, awake when you are.

3

You meet and learn

Video call. Decide what you're working on. Get on with it. Then again next week.

Why I built this

I grew up in a small town. I wanted to learn programming and there was nobody around me who wanted the same thing. Not one person.

I didn't need another course. There are thousands of those. I needed someone working on the same thing at the same time, who'd show up on Tuesday because I was expecting them.

That's all PeerFlow is.
