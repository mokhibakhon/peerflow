The rows menu, cleaned up

You picked the list. It was messy for reasons you can point at, so here it is with those fixed — twice, differing only in how the chosen row is marked.

  • Three type systems in one panel — small caps in a second font, a bold row, a grey date — become one ramp in one font.
  • Three radii (13 outer, 8 row, 7 cell) become one family: 14, 10, 9.
  • Everything now starts on the same 20px gutter. Heading, row and time cell used to each start somewhere slightly different.
  • The chosen row loses the full-width green bar for a tick.
  • Times were filled buttons above the divider and bare text below it — two controls for one job. Now one shape, one quieter than the other.
  • A nub points at the word you pressed, so the panel belongs to it.
