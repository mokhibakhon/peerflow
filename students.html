Community rules

Our code of conduct

PeerFlow works because people feel safe showing up as beginners. That's protected.

Be the peer you'd want beside you

Everyone here is mid-climb. Answer beginner questions the way you wished yours were answered. "Explain it like I'm five" is a compliment on PeerFlow, not an insult.

Show up when you say you will

Sessions only work if seats get filled by people who arrive. Your show-up score is visible for a reason — protect it like a streak.

Zero tolerance for harassment

Live sessions with strangers require trust. Harassment, discrimination, or making anyone feel unwelcome ends your PeerFlow account — first strike. Every session has one-tap report and block.

Hack labs, not people

The cybersecurity track practices on legal platforms — CTFs, intentionally vulnerable labs, and cert material. Discussing attacks on real systems without authorization gets you removed.
