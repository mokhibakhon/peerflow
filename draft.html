The problem

Why it looks wrong

Four things, and they compound. A card inside a card — the green panel wraps a white card, and nowhere else in the app nests two surfaces. Three backgrounds in one component, where every other block is one white card on grey. The green is doing the wrong job — everywhere else in PeerFlow a green fill means achieved: the week you are on, a badge you earned, sessions you have had together. Here it means unread, which is a different idea wearing the same clothes. And the weight is inverted: a whole green field is louder than the top pick, so the page has two things shouting and the recommendation loses.

If you want my opinion

B, and here is why

C is the most consistent and I would pick it for anything else on this page — but this block is the one thing here where somebody else is waiting on you, and C makes it look like furniture. A is nearly as tidy and a single border of change; if you want the smallest possible edit, take A. B keeps one white card like the rest of the app while still reading as "this is addressed to you", and the green band echoes the grey header the People table already has, so it is a shape the page has seen before.

All three fix the real fault, which is the nested card. All three also drop the two-sentence paragraph to one line — that copy was written to fill a panel that is now going away.
