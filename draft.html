Step two — three pictures for “Find a compatible learner”

The picture that ships today shows three names and three buttons. The heading above it promises compatible, and the picture shows nothing about compatibility — no path, no topic, no level, no shared hours. The real directory has all of it. Each option below picks one of those facts and makes it the whole picture.

The right-hand column is the one to judge by. These sit in a 322 px card on the landing page — a quarter of the size they are drawn at. assets/how-frames.html already warns that a faithful screenshot is a grey blur at that width. An option that reads well big and dissolves small is the wrong option.

Names are illustrative, not taken from real accounts — a public landing page should not carry the names of actual members.

One idea: they are learning the thing you are learning.

Closest to what ships today — same three rows, same buttons — with the one badge that answers “compatible”, and the path and stage under each name. Lowest risk, smallest gain.
