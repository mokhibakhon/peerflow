What the calendar leaves out

Re-checking the reschedule work turned up two things in calHourWindow() and the filter that feeds it. The first was losing a booking off the screen. The second was a question about what the grid is for. Both are settled and applied; this page is the record of what was compared.

Fixed

A proposal that was not on the calendar at all

The hour window is built from the earliest and latest hour the week contains, then capped at fourteen rows as a backstop against drawing a full day. The cap used to be hi = lo + 13 — it trimmed the bottom of the window without looking at what was in it. A week holding a 5am booking and a Friday 7pm proposal drew 05:00 to 18:00 and never rendered the Friday cell. The sessions card said the proposal existed; the calendar beside it showed a free evening.

This is the same divergence between the calendar and the session list that the reschedule flow exists to prevent, arriving from the other direction — and it was invisible, because a cell that was never drawn looks exactly like an hour with nothing booked in it.

Empty availability rows are what the cap may take. If the bookings themselves span more than fourteen hours the window stays wider than the cap — the card scrolls internally, so a wide window costs a scroll and a narrow one costs a booking.

Chosen — B

Should a session you have already had stay on the calendar?

Right now it does not. calSessions keeps only confirmed and proposed, so the moment settle_due() flips a finished session to completed it disappears from the grid — mid-week, without anything happening. Page back a week and the calendar is empty even though you studied on the Tuesday.

What makes this a question rather than a bug: .cal-ev.done is fully styled in app.css — grey block, grey bar, soft text — and is currently almost unreachable, which reads like it was meant to be drawn. But the legend under the grid has three keys and none of them is "done", which reads like it was not. Both are in the shipped code, so the intent has to come from you.

Both grids below show the same evening. Monday is the session you had; Friday is the one still waiting on an answer.

The week reads as a week. Two knock-ons: the hour window has to cover finished hours too, and the grid auto-scrolls to its first block — that would have to keep preferring a live one, or opening the page lands you on last Monday.

B was chosen and is applied. calSessions and weekOf both take completed now, the legend has its fourth key, and the grid's auto-scroll prefers the first live block so that a week carrying finished sessions still opens on the thing you have to answer rather than on last Monday.
