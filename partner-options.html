Partner page — five layouts

The same two partners in every one, so the only thing changing is the layout. Tell me a letter. You can mix — "E's information in A's shape" is a fine answer.

A

Row cards

What's there now, tightened: avatar on the left, everything on one line, the action on the right. Calmest of the five and the shortest page.

B

Green edge, with facts

A coloured spine down the side and the facts spelled out underneath — topic, stage, timezone. Looks the most like a product; also the most furniture per person.

C

One list

All of them inside a single card, one row each. Takes the least space and stops two partners looking like two separate announcements. Gets plain quickly.

D

Side by side

A grid rather than a stack, so two partners sit together instead of one above the other. Reads as a set. Wastes width with only one.

E

What's happening with each

Each card answers the question you actually came with: is anything booked with this person, and when are we both free? The others describe who your partner is; this one describes where you're up to with them.
